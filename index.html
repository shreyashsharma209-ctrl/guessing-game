Guess the number

Two players take turns. Each player gets their own secret number between 1 and 100. The player who needs fewer guesses wins.

What the hints mean

  • Burning hot Your guess is within 5 of the number.
  • Hot Your guess is within 10 of the number.
  • Warm Your guess is within 20 of the number.
  • Cold Your guess is more than 20 away.
  • Extra hints After 3 guesses you learn if the number is even or odd. After 5 guesses you learn if it is divisible by 5.
